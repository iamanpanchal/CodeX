# Problem : A3

A3.A personal staff for everyone Wealthy people have assistants, accountants, lawyers and someone to handle errands. Everyone else handles it all alone, badly and late. Agents can now read your mail, manage your calendar, pay your bills, chase your refunds, book your appointments and fill your forms. We want the agent that becomes everyone's chief of staff: trusted with real tasks, careful with money and private data, and good enough that people stop doing this work themselves.
